Sources and reconciliation

Source
ProCyclingStats: official UCI team ranking (rankings/me/ranking-2026-2028, 2026-10-18), each team's per-rider breakdown, and every contributing rider's dated season results, GC leader-jersey points and UCI sanctions
Reconciliation
Our rebuilt best-20 totals (each team's 20 best riders, as the UCI counts) match the team's PCS points page for 29 of 30 teams; the exception is MBH Bank (−3 pts, a tie on 20th place that PCS's page counts twice). Counting every rider, they match the sum of the riders' PCS entries for 29 of 30 teams (Kern Pharma −10 pts: a rider whose sanctions exceed his points, which PCS lists as '-'). The official ranking listing matches our best-20 totals for 4 of 30 teams only: it was captured on 18 Oct before that day's Veneto Classic, Eneco Tour, Chrono des Nations, Clásica Provincia de Chiriquí and Tour de Serbie results were added (see notes).

Notes
10 coverage notes
  • Official listing vs per-team breakdown: 4 of 30 teams agree within 1 pt; 26 differ (breakdown +2,252 pts in total, e.g. UAE +375.1, FDJ +247.9, Red Bull-Bora +194.6). The listing is a snapshot taken during 2026-10-18: our best-20 ledger reproduces it for 32/34 teams with results up to 2026-10-17 plus that day's Tour of Guangxi and Japan Cup, but not yet Veneto Classic, Eneco Tour, Chrono des Nations, Clásica Provincia de Chiriquí and Tour de Serbie (all of 2026-10-18: 5/34); the per-team breakdown pages were fetched later and include every result.
  • 'All riders' totals add every rider's points; the official ranking counts only each team's 20 best riders of the season (the 'best 20' view), so all-rider totals are usually higher.
  • UCI sanctions are subtracted on the day they were imposed (126 sanctions, 2,510 pts across the top 30); a week with a sanction can show a negative gain.
  • 16 riders have more sanction points than points (-297 pts in total): they lower the all-rider total but almost never count among a team's best 20, so a few small teams' best-20 totals exceed their all-rider totals.
  • The UCI 2026 season opened in late 2025: 15 results (716 pts) dated 2025-10-23 to 2025-12-05 count for 2026; the chart starts at the week of 2026-01-12, each line opening at its total so far (those points included), while the data keeps every week from 2025-10-20.
  • GC leader-jersey points (297 awards, 2,562 pts) are not listed on PCS rider result pages; they come from each rider's UCI-ranking page and are dated to the stage after which the jersey was held.
  • 8 riders changed team during the season; their points are split by date between the teams.
  • 275 pts that riders scored for teams outside the ranking (development/continental teams) before a transfer are not in any team total, as on PCS.
  • For 3 teams PCS's team page total differs from the sum of its riders' 'points counted' — Euskaltel +0.71 and MBH Bank +3: two riders tie on 20th place and the page total counts both (the UCI best-20 sum, and ours, counts one); Kern Pharma -10: a rider with more sanction points than points shows '-' but is subtracted. The page total is shown as the breakdown total.
  • Annotations: each team's biggest weeks (normally 3 to 6 per team) plus the weeks of its headline wins — every Monument, elite World title and Grand Tour GC, and WorldTour one-day or stage-race wins up to 10 annotations per team. Labels name the riders and results behind the week ('wins' only for a 1st place; a team time trial is the team's win, not a rider's stage win); in a stage race's final week the label counts the stage wins of the whole race, while the points are that week's.
